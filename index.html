Chord

Windows are paper on a desk, not walls of a room. Keyboard-first window management for macOS.

This is an interactive simulation of Chord's keyboard grammar. The real app is pre-alpha and not installable yet; this page runs the same neighbour picker and Unfold math in your browser. Click the desk, then use your keyboard (Option on a Mac, Alt elsewhere), or use the buttons.

  • Alt+HJKL look: focus moves to the neighbour the picker chooses. Press two keys together for a diagonal.
  • Alt+Shift+HJKL carry the focused window by a few pixels.
  • Alt+1–9 or ` go to a workspace. Add Shift to carry the focused window there.
  • Alt+0 tap: Unfold. The window shrinks to a card, then grows along a smooth path to the largest 16:9 box. Press again mid-way to restart from where it is.

Spread (hold Alt+0) comes later. Source and charter: github.com/jjaguirr/chord
